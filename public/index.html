    Keyboard and mouse

    Click a key to change it, then press the new one. Esc cancels, Backspace clears. Each action can have two keys.

    Controller No controller found. Plug one in (Xbox or PlayStation style) and press any button.

    Left stickmoveRight stickaim RTdraw and fireA LTdash LB RBabilities Q and EStartmenu X Y Bupgrade 1, 2, 3D-padmenus (A select, B back)

    Aim assist is built in for controllers, one strength for everyone. Near an enemy your aim slows and is drawn most of the way toward a spot just ahead of them, and once they're under your aim it follows them. Only a firm push of the stick steers, so easing off to fire keeps your aim. It never locks on, and the mouse is never assisted.
