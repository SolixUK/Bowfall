    Bowfall Duels

    Red against Blue across four arenas. No respawns: the last team standing wins the game. First to 3 games takes the round and a point, and the first team to 3 points wins the match. Pick an element and a role to make your archetype, like Frost Sniper or Poison Juggernaut. Before the first round and after each one, you choose one of three upgrades from your two trees. Amber on the centre line heals you a little and speeds up your cooldowns.

    Practice vs bots

    Set up teams with bots, pick an arena and play. Runs right here, no server needed.

    Play online

    Online matches run on your own Bowfall server. Start it with npm start, open the address it prints, and create a room there. Friends join with the room code.
